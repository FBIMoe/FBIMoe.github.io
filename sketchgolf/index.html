Sketch Golf

A hand-drawn golf game for iPhone and iPad. A new Daily hole every day, the same for everyone.

Help

  • Aiming: drag back from the ball like pulling a slingshot. The further you pull, the harder the shot. Let go to hit.
  • Clubs: pick one in the bar at the bottom, or turn on Auto club in Settings. Straight / Draw / Fade curves the ball.
  • Wind: the tag at the top shows its direction and speed. It pushes the ball while it's in the air.
  • Your holes: draw your own hole, play it, and share it with a code. A friend pastes the code to play the same hole.
  • Where your progress lives: on your device only. It comes back with a device backup; deleting the app deletes it.

Contact

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. A screenshot and a sentence about what happened is plenty.

Privacy

Sketch Golf collects no data: no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
