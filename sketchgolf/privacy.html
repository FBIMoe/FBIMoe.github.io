Sketch Golf

Privacy Policy

Sketch Golf for iPhone and iPad. Effective November 19, 2026.

In short: Sketch Golf collects no data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your scores, settings and the holes you draw stay on your device.

What the app collects

Nothing. Sketch Golf does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. The app does not connect to the internet to send anything about you or how you play.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, scores, stats, achievements and unlocked balls.
  • The holes you draw in the designer, and their names.

These are saved in the app's own storage on your device. They are included in your device backup (iCloud or your computer) the same way as other app data, under Apple's terms. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing a hole

When you share a hole you drew, the app makes a share code containing that hole's drawing and the name you gave it. The code goes only where you choose to send it (for example a message to a friend). Sketch Golf doesn't upload it anywhere.

Apple services

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage figures. That sharing is controlled by you in Settings and handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. If a future version adds Game Center leaderboards, they are Apple's service and also covered by Apple's policy.

Children

Sketch Golf is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever changes what the app collects, this policy will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date at the top, and the App Store privacy details will be updated to match.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
